## The short version

Lok Sewa Sathi does not collect your personal data. There is no account, no
sign-up, and nothing you do in the app is sent to us or to anyone else.

## What the app stores

The app keeps the following **on your own device only**:

- Which post you are preparing for
- Your chosen interface language
- Your quiz and mock test results, and per-subject accuracy
- Notes and questions you bookmark
- Any PDF you add on the Materials screen, together with the title you gave it
  and the paper you filed it under

This is held in the app's own private storage. We cannot read it. It is not
backed up to any server we control, and uninstalling the app deletes it. A PDF
you add is never uploaded — it is read only to show it back to you, and
removing it from the Materials screen deletes the file from your device.

## What the app does not do

- No account or login
- No analytics or usage tracking
- No advertising, and no advertising identifiers
- No location, contacts, camera, microphone or file access
- No personal data collected, transmitted or sold

## Network use

**The Android app** ships its study material inside the app. It does not need a
network connection to work and does not transmit your activity.

**The web version**, if you use it, requests fonts from Google Fonts
(`fonts.googleapis.com`, `fonts.gstatic.com`). Like any web request, this makes
your IP address visible to Google. Google's handling of that is governed by
their own privacy policy. The Android app does not make this request.

If the app shows source links for a current-affairs entry and you tap one, your
browser opens that news site, which is then governed by that site's own policy.

## Children

The app is aimed at adults preparing for civil service examinations. It does not
knowingly collect data from anyone, of any age.
