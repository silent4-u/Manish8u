## Full description (4000 characters max)
```
Preparation for the Nepal civil service examinations conducted by the Public
Service Commission (Lok Sewa Aayog), for three posts in the Administration
Service: Section Officer (Shakha Adhikrit), Nayab Subba and Kharidar.

Every word is in both Nepali and English. Switch language whenever you like
without losing your place.

WHAT IS INSIDE

• The first paper in one place
  The objective paper is what every post sits. Pick a topic and study it, with
  each topic showing which posts examine it and for how many marks.

• Syllabus you can actually read
  Every paper with its full marks, pass marks, duration and question pattern,
  broken down section by section and linked to the notes that cover it.

• Study notes built for revision
  Structured lessons with fact grids, comparison tables and key-point callouts,
  each sized for one sitting rather than one long wall of text.

• Practice with explanations
  Ten questions at a time, by subject or mixed. The correct answer and a written
  explanation appear the moment you answer, so a mistake teaches you something.

• Timed mock tests in the real pattern
  Your post's actual question count, duration and marking — negative marking
  included — with a question palette and a full answer review at the end.

• Progress that means something
  Accuracy per subject, attempt history and overall figures, so you can see which
  topic is costing you marks.

• Contemporary issues, month by month
  Filed by month. Anything not yet confirmed against an official source is marked
  as such, with its sources shown, so you are never handed an unchecked claim as
  a settled answer.

• Your own PDFs, filed by paper
  Add a PDF and file it under the paper — and the section of that paper — it
  belongs to, then read it offline. Added files are held on your phone and are
  never uploaded anywhere.

• Bookmarks
  Save any note or question for a last-minute revision list.

WORKS WITHOUT A CONNECTION

Everything is stored on your device. Open it on the bus, in a load-shedding
window, or anywhere the signal drops. No account, no sign-up.

YOUR DATA STAYS YOURS

The app collects nothing. No account, no analytics, no advertising identifiers,
nothing sent to any server. Your progress and any PDF you add live in your
phone's own storage and are deleted when you uninstall.

ABOUT THE CONTENT

Marks, timings and topics follow the Public Service Commission's published
syllabus for the Administration Service. The commission revises its syllabi from
time to time, so always confirm against the latest notice on psc.gov.np before
your examination. This app is study material, not the official syllabus, and it
is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Public Service Commission.
```
