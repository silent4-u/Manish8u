# Five plans for one plot, with the same rooms

The plot in the drawing sent:
- 28'-0" wide;
- 57'-0" deep on the left and 53'-6½" on the right;
- a house 24'-0" × 35'-6", with 3'-0" open on the left, 1'-0" on the right and 17'-4½" open at the front.

The drawing has 2 bedrooms, a living room, a kitchen, a toilet, a verandah and a stair. Each of these five plans keeps the same rooms, the same footprint and the same setbacks, so the approved site layout still works.

| Plan | Idea | Bedrooms | Living | Kitchen | Toilet |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 · Closest to your drawing | A lobby that every room opens off; the stair has its own door | 12'-0" × 14'-0", 12'-0" × 11'-10½" | 10'-1½" × 10'-0" | 10'-1½" × 7'-10½" | 4'-10½" × 7'-7½" |
| 2 · Open family hall | Living, dining and kitchen as one hall | 12'-0" × 11'-0", 12'-0" × 9'-6" | one hall, 10'-1½" × 26'-3" | | 6'-0" × 5'-0" |
| 3 · Bright kitchen-dining at the front | Kitchen with windows on two sides | 12'-0" × 10'-6", 12'-0" × 10'-6" | 10'-1½" × 10'-0" | 12'-0" × 12'-3" | 6'-9" × 5'-6" |
| 4 · Guest living room + private family side | Separate doors for guests and family | 12'-0" × 11'-0", 12'-0" × 11'-6" | 12'-0" × 10'-9" | 10'-1½" × 8'-3" | 6'-9" × 7'-3" |
| 5 · Kitchen-dining at the back | Kitchen in the rear-left corner, with a back door | 12'-0" × 11'-9", 12'-0" × 10'-7½" | 10'-1½" × 10'-0" | 12'-0" × 10'-10½" | 6'-9" × 5'-6" |

In all five:
- The bedrooms stay on the left. The 3'-0" gap there allows proper windows; the 1'-0" gap on the right only allows ventilators.
- Walls are 9" outside and 4½" for partitions.
- Columns are 12" × 12", shown at likely positions; a structural engineer confirms them.

Files:
- `plan-1.png` … `plan-5.png`: one page per plan, with the room list and the reasons for it.
- `all-plans.png`: all five side by side.
- `house-plans.pdf`: everything, ready to print.
