# हिसाब · Teaser (40 seconds)

**तीन दोस्त। तीन ग़लतियाँ। एक हिसाब।**

The teaser for *हिसाब* ([`script.md`](script.md)) runs exactly 40 seconds.
- **The idea:** it counts the three mistakes, "पहली… दूसरी… तीसरी…", as warm memories turn into fire.
- **What it holds back:** it stops before anyone is forgiven, and before Ali's last six.
- **The last thing we hear:** a frightened boy in the dark.

**Every shot is a shot from the film.**
- The 17 clips cost about 300 Flow credits: roughly a week of the free 50 a day.
- Nothing is wasted, because each clip goes into the full film too.
- **Make the teaser first.** It tells you whether the faces stay the same before you spend credits on the other 268 clips.

---

## Shot by shot

**The format:** time in the teaser · how long it stays · the shot from the film · its ingredients.

Each shot's Flow prompt is under it, made as in the film:
- the ingredients (the character images named);
- the prompt;
- the BASE line;
- the LOOK line given in brackets.

The lines for these are at the end of this page.

**Trims:** each clip comes out 8 seconds long, and **Use** says which part to keep.

**Sound:**
- Mute Flow's own audio except where the shot says to keep it.
- The lines are recorded separately and laid over the cut, because 2-second shots are too short to hold a whole spoken line.

## Text cards

- **Font:** a white serif Devanagari typeface (for example, Noto Serif Devanagari).
- **The three "ग़लती" cards:** 1 second each, fading in and out over the picture with a soft glow.
- **The title:** on black.
- **Frame:** full 16:9 (1920 × 1080). `teaser.py --scope` adds 2.39:1 bars, if you want the film's look.

## Prompt lines

Add to every prompt (BASE):
> Cinematic Hindi drama film, shot on 35mm film, realistic Indian faces and natural skin texture, shallow depth of field, gentle film grain. No on-screen text, no subtitles, no background music.

Then the LOOK given in brackets:

| LOOK | Line to add |
| --- | --- |
| FRAME | Cool blue-green tones, soft fluorescent and lamp light, muted colours. |
| PAST | Warm golden nostalgic light, saffron, turquoise and lime-wash colours, early-2000s Ahmedabad. |
| LOVE | Magenta dusk or warm candle-amber light, soft bloom, tender and intimate. |
| NIGHT | Night, flickering orange firelight against deep blue darkness, smoke haze, handheld camera, high contrast. |

The character images (`ISH`, `OMI`, `GOVIND`, `VIDYA`, `ALI`, `MAMA`, `DHIRAJ`) are described in `script.md`, under **Characters**.

## Making the clips and the cut
