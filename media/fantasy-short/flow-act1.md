# Act I in Google Flow, free

Act I of *The Last Flame of Himavar* (0:00–0:40) as six clips, made with
Google Flow's **50 free credits a day**. That covers it in **2 days**.

## Before you start: settings in Flow

- **Aspect ratio:** 16:9 (landscape).
- **Outputs per prompt: 1.** Flow may make 2 by default, which doubles the
  cost.
- **Model:**
  - **Veo 3.1 Lite** (about 10 credits) for the shots with no people.
  - **Veo 3.1 Fast** (about 20 credits) for the shots with characters, using
    their reference images.

  Check the credit cost Flow shows before you press generate.
- Each clip comes out 8 seconds long. I trim them to the script's timings
  when I edit.

## Step 2: the six shots

Paste each prompt as it is. The last line of each is the style; keep it
the same in every shot.

### Day 1 (50 credits)

**Shot 1: the valley** (Lite, no ingredients)
> Slow aerial drone shot gliding forward over a vast moonlit valley of snow peaks at night. Terraced stone villages on the slopes, every window dark. A frozen river winds like a silver thread. Thin clouds drift across a huge full moon. Audio: howling wind and a deep, distant horn. A deep, slow old man's voice-over says: "Every winter, the Sky Lamp keeps the dark from our door."
> Photoreal epic fantasy film, anamorphic lens, shallow depth of field, film grain, cold steel-blue moonlight, warm amber firelight, falling snow, breath vapour. No subtitles.

**Shot 2: the Sky Lamp dies** (Lite, no ingredients)
> A giant bronze brazier the size of a house stands alone on a snowy mountain summit at night, carved with sun runes, frost creeping over the runes, stars behind it. The last embers in its bowl fade to grey and the wind tears away a final curl of smoke. Slow crane shot up from the frosted runes to the dying embers. Audio: wind. A deep, slow old man's voice-over says: "Tonight... it went out."
> Photoreal epic fantasy film, anamorphic lens, shallow depth of field, film grain, cold steel-blue moonlight, warm amber firelight, falling snow, breath vapour. No subtitles.

**Shot 3: the village** (Lite, no ingredients)

Behind them, at the black edge of a pine forest, pairs of glowing ember-red eyes open one by one in the darkness. The camera pushes in past the window towards the eyes. ### Day 2 (50 credits)

**Shot 4: the Hall of Lamps** (Fast; ingredients: Elder Surya, Mira)
> Inside a vast stone monastery hall at night, hundreds of unlit bronze lamps on stepped shelves rise into darkness. A single shaft of moonlight falls through a high window, dust and snow drifting in the beam; monks in grey robes stand silent along the walls. The old monk from the reference image lifts a small brass lantern holding one tiny golden flame, the only warm light in the hall. The young warden from the reference image kneels before him. Slow dolly in, low angle. The old monk says in a deep, grave voice: "This is the last fire of Himavar. Carry it to the summit before dawn."
> Photoreal epic fantasy film, anamorphic lens, shallow depth of field, film grain, cold steel-blue moonlight, warm amber firelight, falling snow, breath vapour. No subtitles.

**Shot 6: Kaal enters** (Fast; ingredient: Kaal)
> In a vast dark stone hall lined with unlit bronze lamps, great iron-studded doors grind open and snow blows in. The scarred armoured man from the reference image walks in towards the camera, his shackle chain dragging and ringing on the stone floor; monks in grey robes turn and whisper. A deep, grave old man's voice says from off screen: "No. With the man who let it die." Audio: grinding doors, wind, the chain on stone.
> Photoreal epic fantasy film, anamorphic lens, shallow depth of field, film grain, cold steel-blue moonlight, warm amber firelight, falling snow, breath vapour. No subtitles.
