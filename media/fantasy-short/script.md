# THE LAST FLAME OF HIMAVAR

*An AI fantasy short film: 3 minutes, 25 shots.*

**Logline.** On the longest night of the year, a young warden must carry the
realm's last living flame up a frozen mountain to relight the Sky Lamp. Her
guide is the disgraced guardian who let it die the winter before.

---

## The look

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Style** | Photoreal epic fantasy, like a big-budget film. The world is Himalayan in feel: snow peaks, terraced stone villages, carved monasteries, wind-torn rune banners. |
| **Frame** | 2.39:1 widescreen (or 16:9 with black bars), 24 fps. Anamorphic lens, shallow depth of field, soft film grain. |
| **Palette** | The world is cold steel blue and moonlit silver. The flame is the only warm colour: amber and gold. The Hollow are ink-black smoke with ember-red eyes. |
| **Atmosphere** | Always on screen: falling snow, breath vapour, drifting mist, volumetric moonlight. |
| **Camera** | Slow dolly and crane moves in the quiet parts, sweeping drone shots for scale, handheld in the fight, macro close-ups on the flame. |

## Characters

Use the same words in every prompt, and the same reference image, so each
character looks the same in every shot.

- **MIRA**, 19, a warden of the Order of the Flame.
  - Slim, determined; warm brown skin, dark hair in a single braid with a
    copper ring, a small ash mark on her forehead.
  - A layered crimson wool robe with brass clasps, a fur-lined grey cloak,
    worn leather gloves.
  - She carries a small brass lantern with a tiny golden flame.
- **KAAL**, 45, a disgraced former Flame-Guardian.
  - Tall and broad; a grey-streaked beard, the sides of his head shaved, a
    scar through his left eyebrow, tired eyes. Old burn scars on both
    palms.
  - Dented dark-iron armour plates over a long worn black coat.
  - A heavy iron shackle on his right wrist, with a broken length of chain
    hanging from it. He carries a broad curved blade.
- **ELDER SURYA**, 70s, head of the Order. A long white beard; saffron and
  gold robes; a wooden staff topped with a dark, empty lamp.
- **THE HOLLOW.** Wolf-sized shapes of black smoke with glowing ember eyes.
  They flinch from light and turn to ash when the flame touches them.

## Places

- **Emberhold.** The Order's monastery-fortress on a cliff edge.
  - Carved grey stone and snow on every ledge.
  - Inside, the Hall of Lamps: hundreds of unlit bronze lamps on stepped
    shelves.
- **Mount Duskara.** The highest peak. On its summit stands the **Sky
  Lamp**, a giant bronze brazier the size of a house, carved with sun runes.
- **The Weeping Gorge.** An old rope bridge of frayed planks over a deep
  gorge, with mist below and wind howling through it.
- **The Ice Valley.** A frozen waterfall of glowing blue ice columns under
  the moon.
- **Tumla.** A village of stone houses on terraced fields. Tonight every
  window is dark.

## Script

### ACT I · THE DARK (0:00–0:40)

**1 · 0:00–0:08 · EXT. HIMAVAR, NIGHT**
- **Background:** A vast moonlit valley of snow peaks. Terraced villages lie
  on the slopes with every window dark. A frozen river winds like silver
  thread. Thin clouds drift across a huge full moon.
- **Camera:** A slow aerial glide forward over the valley.
- **Sound:** A howling wind and a deep, distant horn.
- **VO (Elder Surya, deep and slow):** "Every winter, the Sky Lamp keeps the
  dark from our door."

**2 · 0:08–0:14 · EXT. SUMMIT OF MOUNT DUSKARA, NIGHT**
- **Background:** The giant bronze Sky Lamp stands alone on the summit. Frost
  creeps over its sun runes. The last embers in its bowl fade to grey, and
  a final curl of smoke is torn away by the wind. Stars fill the sky behind
  it.
- **Camera:** A slow crane up from the frosted runes to the dying embers.
- **VO:** "Tonight… it went out."

**3 · 0:14–0:20 · EXT. TUMLA VILLAGE, NIGHT**
- **Background:** Stone houses half-buried in snow, with rune banners frozen
  stiff on their poles.
- **Action:** At a small wooden window, a mother pulls her young daughter
  back from the glass. Behind them, at the black edge of the pine forest,
  pairs of ember-red eyes open one by one.
- **Camera:** Push in past the window towards the eyes.
- **Sound:** Wind chimes clinking, then a low growl.

**4 · 0:20–0:30 · INT. EMBERHOLD, HALL OF LAMPS, NIGHT**
- **Background:** A vast stone hall with hundreds of unlit bronze lamps on
  stepped shelves, rising into darkness. A single shaft of moonlight falls
  through a high window. Dust and snow drift in the beam. Monks in grey
  robes stand silent along the walls.
- **Action:** Elder Surya lifts a small brass lantern. Inside it burns one
  tiny golden flame: the only warm light in the hall. Mira kneels before
  him.
- **Camera:** A slow dolly in, low angle.
- **Elder:** "This is the last fire of Himavar. Carry it to the summit
  before dawn."

**5 · 0:30–0:35 · INT. HALL OF LAMPS, CLOSE-UP**
- **Background:** Darkness behind her, softly out of focus.
- **Action:** The flame is reflected in Mira's eyes, and her breath clouds
  in the cold.
- **Camera:** A macro close-up on her face, the lantern glow on her skin.
- **Mira (quiet):** "Alone?"

**6 · 0:35–0:40 · INT. HALL OF LAMPS, NIGHT**
- **Background:** The great iron-studded doors grind open, and snow blows in.
- **Action:** Kaal walks in, his shackle chain dragging and ringing on the
  stone floor. The monks turn and whisper.
- **Camera:** A wide shot from behind Mira, Kaal walking towards the camera.
- **Elder (off screen):** "No. With the man who let it die."

### ACT II · THE ROAD (0:40–2:00)

**7 · 0:40–0:46 · EXT. EMBERHOLD GATE, BLIZZARD**
- **Background:** A carved stone gateway with torches long dead, and snow
  driving sideways through it.
- **Action:** A monk unlocks the chain from the wall, but the shackle stays
  on Kaal's wrist. Mira and Kaal step out into the storm.
- **Camera:** Handheld, following them through the gate.
- **Kaal:** "Keep it close to your heart. The cold eats fire first."

**8 · 0:46–0:54 · EXT. SNOW RIDGE, NIGHT**
- **Background:** A knife-edge ridge under an enormous moon, with mountain
  ranges fading into blue haze. The lantern's warm dot is the only colour
  in the whole frame.
- **Action:** Two tiny figures walk the ridge in silhouette.
- **Camera:** An extreme wide drone shot, drifting sideways.
- **Music:** A low drum begins, with a cello theme.

**9 · 0:54–1:02 · EXT. THE WEEPING GORGE, ROPE BRIDGE**
- **Background:** A long rope bridge sags over a bottomless gorge. Its
  planks are frayed and iced, and mist boils far below. The wind makes the
  bridge sway.
- **Action:** A gust swings the lantern. Mira slips and a plank snaps under
  her. Kaal's chained hand shoots out and grabs her wrist.
- **Camera:** Starts wide, then a fast handheld push in.
- **Sound:** Creaking rope, a snapping plank, wind.

**10 · 1:02–1:07 · EXT. ROPE BRIDGE, CLOSE-UP**
- **Background:** Swirling mist, softly out of focus.
- **Action:** Their hands locked together, with the iron shackle between
  them. Mira sees old burn scars across his palm.
- **Mira:** "You burned your hands."
- **Kaal:** "Not enough."

**11 · 1:07–1:15 · EXT. THE ICE VALLEY, NIGHT**
- **Background:** A frozen waterfall of tall blue ice columns, glowing in
  the moonlight, with icicles like organ pipes and snow sparkling on the
  ground.
- **Action:** They rest under an ice overhang. Kaal opens his coat to shield
  the lantern from the wind.
- **Camera:** A slow orbit around them.
- **Mira:** "Why did you let it go out?"

**12 · 1:15–1:22 · FLASHBACK: TUMLA, LAST WINTER**
- **Look:** Washed out, warm, slightly blurred at the edges, like a memory.
- **Background:** A burning house in the village. Behind it, high on the
  mountain, the Sky Lamp burns.
- **Action:** A younger Kaal, in clean armour, turns his back on the summit
  and runs into the flames. He carries a crying child out of the fire.
  Above, the Sky Lamp flickers and dims.
- **Kaal (VO):** "A child was trapped in the fire. I chose her… and the
  realm paid."

**13 · 1:22–1:27 · EXT. ICE VALLEY, PRESENT**
- **Background:** The blue glow of the ice on their faces.
- **Action:** Mira looks at him for a long moment, softening. Kaal looks
  away.
- **Camera:** A close two-shot, very still.
- **Mira:** "Then the realm owes her life to you."

**14 · 1:27–1:35 · EXT. ICE VALLEY, NIGHT**
- **Background:** The shadows between the ice columns begin to move.
- **Action:** Dozens of ember-red eyes open in the darkness all around.
  Black-smoke wolves (the Hollow) slide out of the shadows. The lantern
  flame thrashes as they close in.
- **Camera:** A slow pull back that reveals they are surrounded.
- **Music:** A sharp string hit, then silence.

**15 · 1:35–1:45 · EXT. ICE VALLEY, THE FIGHT**
- **Background:** Snow and sparks whirling; the blue ice lit by flashes of
  gold.
- **Action:** Kaal draws his curved blade and swings his chain like a whip.
  Mira raises the lantern high. Wherever its light falls, the Hollow shriek
  and burst into ash.
- **Camera:** Fast handheld with quick cuts; slow motion on the bursts of
  ash.
- **Music:** Driving war drums.

**16 · 1:45–1:52 · EXT. ICE VALLEY**
- **Action:** One huge Hollow leaps straight at the lantern. Kaal throws
  himself into its path. Its claws rake across his armour, and he smashes
  it apart with the chain. He staggers, bleeding.
- **Camera:** Slow motion as the claws strike, then back to real speed.

**17 · 1:52–2:00 · EXT. THE LAST STAIRWAY, NIGHT**
- **Background:** Ancient stone steps cut into the ice, climbing towards
  the summit, with stars wheeling above. The Sky Lamp's dark silhouette sits
  at the top.
- **Action:** Mira half carries the wounded Kaal up the steps, the lantern
  in her other hand.
- **Camera:** A low angle looking up the stairway.

### ACT III · THE LAMP (2:00–3:00)

**18 · 2:00–2:08 · EXT. SUMMIT OF MOUNT DUSKARA, NIGHT**
- **Background:** Howling wind; the giant dark Sky Lamp. Below them, a
  black tide of Hollow is pouring up the mountain slopes like smoke
  climbing a chimney.
- **Camera:** A wide crane shot rising over the summit.

**19 · 2:08–2:14 · SUMMIT, CLOSE-UP**
- **Action:** Mira opens the lantern. A gust of wind hits it, and the tiny
  flame shrinks to a single spark, then dies. A thin thread of smoke rises.
- **Camera:** A macro shot of the empty lantern.
- **Sound:** Total silence, with only a heartbeat.

**20 · 2:14–2:20 · SUMMIT**
- **Action:** Mira stares at the empty lantern in despair. Kaal, on his
  knees and bleeding, looks up at her.
- **Kaal:** "The flame was never in the lantern, warden. It is in the one
  who carries it."

**21 · 2:20–2:30 · SUMMIT**
- **Action:** Mira pulls off her glove and presses her bare palm to the
  frozen bronze. She closes her eyes. Her breath begins to glow gold, and
  lines of light race through the carved runes like veins of fire.
- **Background:** At the top of the stairway, Kaal stands one last time,
  holding back the Hollow with his chain. He is a silhouette against the
  glow.
- **Music:** A choir swells.

**22 · 2:30–2:40 · SUMMIT, THE LAMP IGNITES**
- **Action:** The Sky Lamp bursts alight. A pillar of golden fire shoots
  into the sky, and a ring of light sweeps down the mountain. The Hollow
  turn to ash and scatter like snow.
- **Camera:** A wide shot, then a fast drone pull back to show the whole
  mountain lit.
- **Music:** The full orchestra hits.

**23 · 2:40–2:48 · EXT. TUMLA VILLAGE, DAWN**
- **Background:** Golden light rolls across the valley.
- **Action:** One by one, every window in Tumla lights up. At the small
  window, the mother and her daughter are bathed in gold, and the girl
  smiles.
- **Camera:** An aerial sweep over the rooftops down to the window.

**24 · 2:48–2:55 · SUMMIT, SUNRISE**
- **Background:** A pink and gold dawn breaking over the peaks; snow
  glittering.
- **Action:** Mira kneels beside Kaal. The iron shackle on his wrist has
  melted away in the fire, and only a glowing ring of iron lies in the
  snow. He is alive, and smiles faintly.
- **Kaal:** "Keep it close… to your heart."
- **Mira:** "Always."

**25 · 2:55–3:00 · FINAL WIDE**
- **Background:** The Sky Lamp burning against the sunrise, with the whole
  realm below waking in golden light.
- **Title card:** **THE LAST FLAME OF HIMAVAR**

## Making it with AI video (Seedance, Kling)

1. **Character sheets first.** Make one reference image each of Mira, Kaal,
   the Elder and the Hollow, from the descriptions above. Use them as
   reference images in every shot, so the characters look the same
   throughout.
2. **One clip per shot.** Most tools make clips of about 5–10 seconds, which
   fits these shots. For each one, combine the Background, Action and
   Camera lines into the prompt, then add the style:

   > *Photoreal epic fantasy film, anamorphic 2.39:1, shallow depth of
   > field, film grain, cold blue moonlight, warm amber firelight, falling
   > snow, breath vapour.*

3. **Several takes.** Generate 2–3 takes of each shot and keep the best.
4. **Voices and music.** Record the dialogue and voice-over separately (your
   own voices, or an AI voice), and lay the music over the edit in the
   order of the table above.
5. **Finish in the edit.** Use the same colour grade on every clip. Add the
   title card at the end, and the black bars if you made the shots in 16:9.
